Kynlee Walker is a Provo, Utah based artist who works with vibrant, monochromatic color in painting, prints, and sculptures. In her work, she wrestles with inner thanksgiving and perspective. Her work has been featured in various collaborative shows at Brigham Young University and local group installations, including the Provo Public Library. Kynlee has had the opportunity to study art around the world, including Japan, Washington DC, and Brazil. She is the two time recipient of BYU Fine Arts 2D Talent Scholarship, the Will Ward–Visual Arts Scholarship, and third place winner of the Mayhew Show contest. Kynlee was born in 2003 in Memphis, Tennessee and is currently working towards a BFA from Brigham Young University.
